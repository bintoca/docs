## Copyright and DMCA Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

We respect the intellectual property rights of others and expect users of bintoca to do the same. This policy explains how to report claimed copyright infringement under the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), how we respond, and how we treat repeat infringers.

### How to Report Copyright Infringement

To be effective under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3), your written notice must include substantially all of the following:

1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or, if multiple works are covered by a single notice, a representative list of those works.
3. Identification of the material that is claimed to be infringing or to be the subject of infringing activity, with information reasonably sufficient to permit us to locate the material (for example, a URL, workspace name, and file path).
4. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf.

If your notice does not include all of this information, we may be unable to act on it.

Before submitting a notice, please consider whether the use may be authorized by the copyright owner, permitted by a license, or protected by fair use or another legal exception. We cannot provide legal advice about whether a particular use is infringing.

### What Happens After We Receive a Notice

When we receive a notice that substantially complies with the DMCA, we may:

- remove or disable access to the identified material;
- notify the affected user and provide a copy of the notice, unless we are prohibited from doing so by law; and
- forward a copy of the notice to the [Lumen Database](https://lumendatabase.org/) for publication, with personal information removed where appropriate.

We may also remove or disable access to material in response to notices that do not fully comply with the DMCA if the claim is otherwise clear.

### Counter-Notice

If your material was removed or disabled and you believe the removal was the result of mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notice to our designated agent. To be effective under 17 U.S.C. §512(g)(3), your counter-notice must include substantially all of the following:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before it was removed or disabled.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, mailing address, and telephone number.
5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district in which your address is located (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which bintoca may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who provided the original notice or that person's agent.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we will provide a copy to the person who submitted the original notice and may restore the material in 10 to 14 business days, unless that person notifies us that they have filed a court action seeking to restrain the allegedly infringing activity.

### Repeat Infringers

We maintain a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, the accounts of users who are repeat infringers. We may also suspend or terminate an account, or remove content, for a single act of infringement in appropriate circumstances. We keep records of infringement notices, counter-notices, removals, and related account actions in order to apply this policy consistently.

### Misrepresentations

Knowingly making a material misrepresentation in a notice or counter-notice may subject you to liability under 17 U.S.C. §512(f), including liability for the other party's costs and attorneys' fees. Do not submit a notice or counter-notice unless you have a good-faith belief that the information is accurate.

### Other Intellectual Property Claims

The DMCA process applies to claims of copyright infringement. For trademark, patent, or other intellectual property concerns, contact [support@bintoca.com](mailto:support@bintoca.com) with the subject line "Intellectual Property Complaint" and include the same identifying information described above, along with the basis for your claim.

### No Legal Advice

We cannot provide legal advice to users, whether they are making a complaint or responding to one. If you have legal questions about copyright, infringement, fair use, or the DMCA process, please consult an attorney.
